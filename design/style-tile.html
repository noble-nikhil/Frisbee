7 · What we don't do

The fastest tells of a generated UI. Reviewers will check PRs against this list.

  • Gradient backgrounds, gradient text, gradient buttons
  • Glassmorphism / backdrop-blur panels
  • Glow or coloured drop shadows
  • Pill-shaped cards (radius > 8 px)
  • Animated blobs, particles, floating shapes
  • Emoji in headings, buttons, empty states
  • Hero stats counters ("10k+ students")
  • Fake testimonials and logo walls
  • Icon-only buttons without labels (except top bar)
  • Purple. Anything purple.
  • Three accent colours on one screen
  • Page-load entrance animations
  • Exclamation marks in UI copy
  • Centered body text longer than one line
