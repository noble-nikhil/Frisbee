Why campuses need frisbee

Friendship used to happen by accident. Now it has to be designed.

Market · students in higher education
269million

worldwide, across 22,000+ institutions — more than double the year 2000. 4.5 crore of them are in India, 71 % in private colleges.

UNESCO Higher Education Global Trends Report 2026 (2024 data) · AISHE 2023-24, Ministry of Education
Problem · students who feel lonely
1 in 2

52 % report high loneliness (Healthy Minds, 84,000 students); 57 % lonely sometimes or always (Trellis, 44,000). Worldwide, 19–29-year-olds are the loneliest age group — lonelier than people over 65.

Healthy Minds Study 2024-25 · Trellis Strategies 2026 · Meta-Gallup Global State of Social Connections, 142 countries
Cost · belonging decides who graduates
30%

of students who quit for personal reasons say it was because they felt alone or isolated. Each 1-point gain in belonging lifts 4-year graduation by +3.4 pts — rivalling thousands of dollars in aid.

Trellis Strategies 2026 · Brady, Educational Researcher (AERA) 2025, N = 21,700 · Lumina–Gallup 2025: 32 % consider dropping out every 6 months
Why now · in-person time with friends, ages 15–24
−70%

From ~150 minutes a day in 2003 to ~40 minutes in 2020. 51 % of people say making new friends is difficult — college students included. The meeting points have to be rebuilt.

U.S. Surgeon General advisory 2023 (time-use data) · American Friendship Project, PLOS ONE 2024
