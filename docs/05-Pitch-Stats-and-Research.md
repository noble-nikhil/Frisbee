# frisbee — Pitch research: why campuses need this

**Purpose:** give the pitch 4 headline statistics that survive a judge asking "where's that from?", plus a bank of supporting numbers per module, a market-sizing slide, and a 2-minute campus survey so you can add a first-party stat from SRM AP itself.

Every number below has: the source, year, sample size, a link, and a "judge-proofing" note. Say the numbers exactly as written — rounding is fine, inflating is not.

## The 4 headline stats (use these on the "Why" slides)

### 1 · The market — 269 million students, and India is the second-largest pool

> **269,000,000** students are enrolled in higher education worldwide — more than double the 100 million of 2000 — across 22,000+ institutions. **4.5 crore (45 million)** of them are in India.

- **What it proves:** the problem isn't niche; every one of these students goes through the same "new campus, no network" moment.
- **Sources:**
  - UNESCO, *Higher Education Global Trends Report* (May 2026, data for 2024, 146 countries): 269 M enrolled, 43 % of the college-age population, 22,000+ accredited institutions. https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/transforming-higher-education-global-roadmap-future · https://thepienews.com/global-he-enrolment-reaches-269-million-as-student-mobility-triples/
  - AISHE 2023-24 (Ministry of Education, released July 2026): 4,50,01,123 students, GER 30, ~3.5 crore undergraduates (76.8 %), 59,533 institutions; private colleges enrol 71.4 % of all college students. https://www.indiatoday.in/education-today/news/story/aishe-2023-24-higher-education-enrolment-india-4-5-crore-private-colleges-lead-2943799-2026-07-09 · https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/indias-higher-education-enrolment-hits-45-crore-women-marginalised-groups-lead-way-aishe-report/article71199024.ece
- **Say it like this:** "There are 269 million university students on the planet and 45 million in India alone. Every single one of them arrives on a campus where they know nobody."
- **Judge-proofing:** if someone quotes 264 M, that's UNESCO's June 2025 figure (for 2023); 269 M is the 2024 figure from the 2026 report. Both are UNESCO.

### 2 · The problem — more than half of students are lonely, and young adults are the loneliest people on Earth

> **52–57 %** of college students report loneliness. Globally, **19–29-year-olds are the loneliest age group** (27 % "very or fairly lonely" — higher than people over 65).

- **What it proves:** the need is universal and measured, not anecdotal; and it peaks exactly at university age.
- **Sources:**
  - Healthy Minds Study 2024-25 (University of Michigan; 84,000+ students, 135 institutions — the largest student mental-health study in the US): **52 %** report high loneliness (58 % in 2022). https://sph.umich.edu/news/2025posts/college-student-mental-health-third-consecutive-year-improvement.html
  - Trellis Strategies, *Invisible on Campus* (Feb 2026; 2024 Student Financial Wellness Survey, ~44,000 students): **57 %** feel lonely (45 % sometimes, 12 % always); only 15 % never; **60 % among students under 25**. https://www.luminafoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/invisible-on-campus-the-student-loneliness-crisis-1.pdf
  - Meta-Gallup, *Global State of Social Connections* (2023; 142 countries, ~1,000 per country, 2,006 in India): 24 % of the world very/fairly lonely; **ages 19–29 highest at 27 %**, 65+ lowest at 17 %. **India: 29 %** very or fairly lonely (14 % + 15 %), 57 % at least a little lonely. https://news.gallup.com/opinion/gallup/512618/almost-quarter-world-feels-lonely.aspx · https://english.gujaratsamachar.com/news/national/india-ranks-36th-in-the-countries-with-most-individuals-self-reported-loneliness
  - Active Minds × TimelyCare (May 2024, US): **64.7 %** of college students report feeling lonely; lonely students are **4.3×** more likely to have high psychological distress. https://assets.activeminds.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/05-10-22024_Report_Call-For-Campus-Action_A4_Final.pdf
  - Wonkhe / Trendence UK (2019): **1 in 6 students (17 %) say they have no true friends at university**; 20 % among international students; nearly a third lonely every week. https://www.the-independent.com/news/education/education-news/student-lonely-university-friends-mental-health-research-a8839011.html
- **Say it like this:** "Roughly one in two students on any campus is lonely right now. And it's not a Western thing — Gallup measured 142 countries and found the loneliest people on Earth are 19- to 29-year-olds. That is exactly who sits in an Indian classroom."
- **Judge-proofing:** the 52 % / 57 % / 65 % spread comes from different scales (UCLA-3 "high loneliness" vs. "sometimes or always" vs. any loneliness). Quote **"more than half"** and you're safe with all three. Don't say "61 % of young adults are seriously lonely (Harvard)" — that's October 2020 pandemic data.

### 3 · The cost — loneliness makes students quit; belonging makes them graduate

> **30 %** of students who dropped out for personal reasons say it was because they **felt alone or isolated**. Every 1-point rise in sense of belonging = **+3.4 percentage points** in four-year graduation — an effect that "rivals thousands of dollars of financial aid".

- **What it proves:** connection is a retention lever, which is how you sell frisbee to a university administration, not just to students.
- **Sources:**
  - Trellis Strategies (2026; national *Some College, No Credential* survey, ~3,000 leavers): among those who left for personal reasons (n = 1,060), 58 % cited emotional/mental-health challenges and **30 % said they stopped out because they felt alone or isolated**. Lonely students are ~**4×** as likely to show depression symptoms (48 % vs 12 %) and rate their institution far lower (NPS 10.9 vs 29.2). Same PDF as above.
  - Brady, *College Students' Sense of Belonging: A Graduation Update*, Educational Researcher / AERA (Nov 2025; N = 21,700, nationally representative): +1 point on the 5-point belonging scale → **+3.4 pp four-year** and **+2.7 pp six-year** attainment. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3102/0013189X251393248 · https://www.insidehighered.com/news/student-success/life-after-college/2026/01/06/increased-sense-belonging-boosts-student
  - Lumina Foundation–Gallup, *State of Higher Education 2025* (Oct 2024 fieldwork, 13,933 adults): **32 %** of enrolled students considered stopping out in the past six months; top reasons **emotional stress (49 %)** and **mental health (41 %)** — ahead of cost (24 %). https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2025/05/02/college-students-consider-dropping-out-2025/
  - EAB (2024 fieldwork, 12,600+ first-year students; two-year-college subsample): the **#1 disappointment** with college was **social life (42 %)**, followed by *not making friends or meeting new people*; **35 %** felt they didn't belong. https://www.insidehighered.com/news/student-success/college-experience/2025/10/08/community-college-students-want-social-life
- **Say it like this:** "A third of students think about quitting every semester, and the top reason isn't money — it's stress and isolation. Universities spend crores on scholarships to fix retention; the research says a one-point gain in belonging does what thousands of dollars in aid does."
- **Judge-proofing:** the 30 % figure is *among students who left for personal reasons*, not among all drop-outs — say it precisely. Brady's data is correlational (she says so); the randomised belonging-intervention trials she cites (Walton et al. 2023) point the same way.

### 4 · Why now — in-person friendship time has collapsed by ~70 %

> Young people (15–24) went from **~150 minutes a day** with friends in person in 2003 to **~40 minutes** in 2020 — a **near-70 % drop**. Students today spend about 70 % less time with peers in person than two decades ago.

- **What it proves:** the default way friendships used to form (unstructured time) is gone; something has to deliberately re-create the meeting points. That's the hangouts / events / groups half of frisbee.
- **Sources:**
  - U.S. Surgeon General, *Our Epidemic of Loneliness and Isolation* (May 2023 advisory, American Time Use Survey data). https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/an-epidemic-of-loneliness-threatenes-health-of-americans-surgeon-general-says-180982142/ · https://www.vox.com/policy/2023/5/3/23707936/surgeon-general-loneliness-epidemic-report
  - Trellis Strategies' Allyson Cornett, quoted Feb 2026: students spend about 70 % less time with peers in person than two decades ago. https://www.insidehighered.com/news/student-success/health-wellness/2026/02/11/student-loneliness-new-normal
  - American Friendship Project (PLOS ONE, 2024; national adults + college students at three universities): **51 %** say it is difficult to make new friends; 62 % say it was easier at another time in life; college students matched adults. https://newsmediarelations.colostate.edu/2024/07/30/are-americans-suffering-a-friendship-crisis-study-shows-its-not-that-we-need-more-friends-just-more-time-with-those-we-already-have/
- **Say it like this:** "Twenty years ago a student spent two and a half hours a day just hanging out with friends. Today it's forty minutes. Friendship used to happen by accident; now it has to be designed. frisbee designs it."
- **Judge-proofing:** the time-use number is US data. Pair it with the Meta-Gallup India number (29 % very/fairly lonely) so it doesn't sound imported.

**Illustrative extrapolations (label them as such on the slide):**
- 27 % (global 19–29 loneliness rate) × 269 M ≈ **~73 million lonely university students worldwide**.
- 29 % (India very/fairly lonely) × 4.5 crore ≈ **~1.3 crore lonely students in India**; at the US campus rate (52 %) it would be ~2.3 crore.
- On a 2,300-student campus, "one in two" = **~1,100–1,300 students** who would say they're lonely if asked today.

## Add a first-party stat: the 2-minute SRM AP survey

Nothing beats "we asked 200 students on this campus last week." Run this as a Google Form for 48 hours (hostel WhatsApp groups, class groups, a QR code in the canteen). Report `n` and the date on the slide.

1. Year of study · Department *(dropdown)*
2. In your first month on campus, how easy was it to find people who share your interests? *(1 very hard – 5 very easy)*
3. How often do you feel lonely on campus? *(Never / Rarely / Sometimes / Often / Always)* ← matches the Trellis wording so you can compare.
4. Have you ever wanted to do something (sport, movie, jam, trip to Vijayawada) but didn't because you had no one to go with? *(Yes / No)*
5. Have you ever struggled to find teammates for a hackathon or project? *(Yes / No / Haven't tried)*
6. Which of these would you use? *(multi-select: find people with shared skills · skill swap · hangouts · events · ride/auto pooling · "bring it for me" errands · peer tutoring · scribe/volunteer support · communities)*
7. *(Optional)* Would you pay a verified senior ₹200–400/hr for tutoring? *(Yes / Maybe / No)*

Slide format: "**n = 214 SRM AP students, Sep 2026:** 6x % feel lonely sometimes or more · 7x % skipped an activity for lack of company · 5x % struggled to find a hackathon team." Put frisbee's module list next to it — each pain maps to a module.

## Things not to say (they will get challenged)

- "61 % of young adults are lonely (Harvard)" — that's an October 2020, n≈950 pandemic snapshot. Use Healthy Minds / Trellis / Meta-Gallup instead.
- Any number from Reddit, Quora, or an SEO "statistics 2026" blog without a primary source.
- "Loneliness epidemic among Indian students: X %" — there is no national Indian student-loneliness survey; use Meta-Gallup's India figure (29 % very/fairly lonely, all ages) or the NIMHANS mental-health study, and be explicit about what each measures.
- Mixing "sometimes lonely" (57 %) with "seriously lonely" (12 %). Keep the scale in the sentence.
- Presenting the extrapolations (73 M, 1.3 crore) as measured facts — label them "estimate".

## One-paragraph version (for the deck's speaker notes or the Devfolio write-up)

There are 269 million students in higher education worldwide and 4.5 crore in India (UNESCO 2024; AISHE 2023-24). More than half of them are lonely: 52 % in the 84,000-student Healthy Minds Study, 57 % in Trellis's 44,000-student survey, and Gallup's 142-country study found 19–29-year-olds are the loneliest age group on Earth. It costs them their degrees — a third of students consider dropping out every six months, with stress and isolation ahead of cost, and 30 % of students who quit for personal reasons say it was because they felt alone — while each one-point gain in belonging lifts four-year graduation by 3.4 points. The cause is structural: in-person time with friends among 15–24-year-olds fell by roughly 70 % between 2003 and 2020. Friendship used to happen by accident. frisbee makes it happen on purpose: structured skill and interest matching, hangouts, events, communities, ride pooling, errands, verified peer tutoring and accessibility volunteering — one installable app for the whole campus.
